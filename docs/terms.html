What the app is

DailyDivine offers a daily verse, an alarm, a reflection journal, streaks and an optional daily wallpaper for personal, non-commercial use.

Alarms and reminders

The app uses Android's alarm features, but phones can delay or block background activity (for example through battery-saving settings), and a phone that is switched off or in a restricted mode cannot ring. Please do not rely on the app alone for anything time-critical, such as a medical or travel commitment.

Daily wallpaper

If you turn on the Daily Wallpaper feature, the app replaces your home and/or lock screen wallpaper with the day's verse once a day. It cannot restore the wallpaper you had before. You can turn the feature off at any time; the last wallpaper then stays until you change it in your phone's settings. Use only photos you have the right to use.
