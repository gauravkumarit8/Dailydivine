## Full description (max 4000)
```
Start each morning with a verse from your own tradition.

DailyDivine is a gentle daily devotional and alarm. It gives you one verse a day, wakes you with a calm tone, and keeps everything private on your phone.

WHAT YOU GET
• A verse every day, with an optional morning notification at a time you choose
• A peaceful alarm: eleven calm tones (bells, chimes, flute, harp, nature sounds), your choice of weekdays, snooze, and "Wake up & read"
• Read aloud: hear the verse spoken after you wake, using your phone's own text-to-speech voice
• Streaks and badges: a quiet nudge to return each day, with milestone celebrations
• A private reflection journal with a mood, and a history of past days
• Daily verse wallpaper (optional): put the day's verse on your home or lock screen, on a calm gradient or your own photo
• Library: browse verses by category, search, and save favourites
• Share a verse as a clean image

YOUR TRADITION
Choose Hinduism, Christianity, Islam or Buddhism. You can change this at any time in Settings.
• Hinduism: The Song Celestial (Bhagavad-Gita), Edwin Arnold, 1885
• Christianity: King James Bible
• Islam: the Quran, Pickthall translation, 1930
• Buddhism: the Dhammapada, Max Müller, 1881
Verses come from public-domain English translations, so the wording can feel old-fashioned. The passages are a gentle selection, not a religious authority. More traditions and languages are planned.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• The app does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
• Clear all your data any time from Settings, or simply uninstall.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Phones can delay or block background apps. If your alarm or wallpaper is late, the app shows a tip for your battery settings.
• The daily wallpaper replaces your current wallpaper once a day while it is switched on, and cannot restore the old one.
• DailyDivine is an independent app and is not affiliated with any religious organisation.

Questions? Write to the address on this page.
```
